# Code of conduct

We want technical disagreement to produce stronger evidence and safer
software. Participants are expected to be precise, patient, inclusive, and
respectful; critique work and claims rather than people; credit sources; protect
private reports and personal information; and make room for newcomers to ask
basic questions.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in
project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when conduct harms the community. Enforcement should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid retaliation or conflicts of
interest. Urgent abuse on GitHub may also be reported through GitHub's
platform-level reporting tools. For a private project-specific report, use
[the repository's private report form](https://github.com/omar07ibrahim/runnelmoe/security/advisories/new)
with a title beginning `conduct:`; this route is also used for confidentiality
even when the report is not a software vulnerability. The repository owner
will restrict the report to people needed for fair review.
